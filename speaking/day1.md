# Overview of Vietnam's Economy and Market Amid Global Volatility and Impacts from the Energy Crisis

## Summary  
In the context of global geopolitical and economic volatility, Vietnam is maintaining remarkable economic stability compared to many other countries in the region. Despite rising energy prices and oil crises occurring in many places, Vietnam, thanks to policies supporting fuel prices and the strong development of domestic enterprises, especially Vingroup Corporation, has maintained growth momentum and the vitality of the stock market. External factors such as strategic petroleum reserves (SPR) of China and Japan, along with strong U.S. oil exports, have helped delay negative impacts until the end of summer. However, Vietnam needs to improve energy infrastructure, develop oil reserves, and prepare to respond to the extreme weather phenomenon El Niño, which could adversely affect agriculture and livelihoods. Balancing investment between traditional energy and renewable energy is also an important lesson for Vietnam in the near future.

## Highlights  
- 🛢️ China has stockpiled about 2 billion barrels of oil, using "oil diplomacy" to increase influence in the region.  
- 📈 Vietnam's stock market reached a peak thanks to Vingroup shares making up nearly 30% of the VN-Index.  
- ☀️ The risk of the strongest El Niño in hundreds of years could cause major volatility in agriculture and food prices.  
- 🇺🇸 The U.S. is strongly exporting oil, despite reduced drilling rigs, along with record releases from strategic petroleum reserves to ease the global energy crisis.  
- 💰 Construction material prices rose by 30%, mortgage borrowing costs increased, making it difficult for people to buy homes, while rental prices soared.  
- ⚡ Vietnam needs to develop more refining capacity and oil reserves to strengthen energy security instead of focusing only on renewable energy.  
- 🏗️ Although infrastructure construction speed has increased, cost and labor pressures are pushing the economy’s absorption capacity to its limits.

## Insights  
- 🛢️ **Strategic Petroleum Reserves of China and Japan – A long-term energy security strategy:** China, with about 2 billion barrels of reserves—far exceeding previous predictions—is using oil as a regional diplomatic tool to protect production interests and enhance political influence. Vietnam, with reserves of only about 10 days compared to China’s monthly reserves, needs to build larger strategic reserves and domestic refining capacity to reduce dependency and flexibly respond to oil price fluctuations.  
- 📈 **The internal strength of Vietnam’s stock market and Vingroup’s key role:** Despite foreign capital continuing to withdraw, domestic individual investors have “supported” the market. Growth is mainly focused on VinGroup stocks with key sectors such as real estate and VinFast electric vehicles. The significant influence of a few companies highlights risks if the market base is not diversified, while also showing strong domestic vitality during global difficulties.  
- 🌡️ **The impact of El Niño on Vietnam’s economy and forecasting lessons:** A strong El Niño can raise sea temperatures by 3-4°C above normal, causing extreme climate changes, agricultural losses, and increased input costs such as fertilizer, pushing up food inflation. Vietnam needs to proactively prepare essential goods, increase food reserves, and develop support plans for farmers to minimize negative impacts.  
- 🔥 **Construction costs and labor shortages in the construction sector:** Rising construction material prices (about 30%) combined with labor shortages are causing construction activity to reach a point where further expansion is difficult despite high demand. This is a sign that the government needs to consider stimulation policies for credit, loan interest rates, and support for the real estate market to avoid cost surges and bubble risks.  
- 💡 **Leveraging traditional and renewable energy: The need for coexistence:** While renewable energy and electric vehicles are seen as the future trend, the current oil crisis reinforces that energy security still largely depends on oil and gas (such as LNG). Vietnam should expand refining infrastructure, reserves, and renewable energy policies to balance sustainability and the environment.  
- 📊 **Global and Vietnamese economic trends before stagflation crisis:** The U.S. is experiencing rising inflation alongside slowing economic growth, creating a volatile investment environment. Meanwhile, Vietnam maintains stable growth thanks to supportive policies and economic resilience. This is a critical time for Vietnam’s government to maintain stability policies and continue improving the investment environment.  
- 🌍 **Geopolitical impacts and international relations:** The China-U.S. meeting amid tensions in the Middle East shows a focus on economics and trade rather than direct confrontation. With stable relations with China and U.S. support through investment and diplomatic programs, Vietnam has opportunities to elevate its role in the global value chain.

## Conclusion  
Vietnam is at a stage of both coping and adapting to complex fluctuations in the global oil market and international supply chains. Domestic strength, stable policies, and enhanced energy security awareness are key factors helping sustain economic growth and keep the stock market attractive. However, risks from El Niño, construction cost pressures, real estate market issues, and the need to balance traditional and green energy require policymakers to implement comprehensive, long-term, and flexible strategies to maintain sustainable development momentum.

## Key Highlights  
- 🛢️ China increases oil reserves beyond expectations, leveraging "oil diplomacy" to maintain regional influence.  
- 📊 Vietnam’s stock market reaches record highs, mainly due to Vingroup shares and active participation from domestic individual investors.  
- 🌡️ The strongest El Niño in centuries may cause massive damage to agriculture, raising food prices and production input costs.  
- 🇺🇸 The U.S. boosts oil exports and draws down reserves to curb the global energy crisis, delaying shortage risks until late summer.  
- 🔨 Rising material prices and labor shortages in construction increase pressure on the real estate market, making housing less accessible.  
- ⚡ Vietnam needs to develop both renewable energy and traditional refining infrastructures and oil reserves to ensure sustainable energy security.  
- 🌍 Geopolitics surrounding the oil crisis and high-level U.S.-China talks significantly impact Vietnam’s economic outlook and strategies.

## In-depth Assessments  
- 🛢️ **China and Japan’s oil reserves: A major energy security lesson for Vietnam.** China’s massive oil stockpiling serves as a political-economic tool, while Vietnam’s very limited reserves create continuous supply disruption risks. Rapid investment in refining capacity and strategic reserves is urgent.  
- 📈 **Domestic market strength and the role of Vingroup’s Vietnamese version.** Vingroup shares strongly support VN-Index, accounting for nearly 30% of market capitalization. This is both a foundation and a warning about excessive dependence on a few “leading drivers” in the market ecosystem.  
- 🌡️ **The return of extreme weather risk (El Niño) imposes dual pressure on the economy and food security.** A very strong El Niño may cause droughts, floods, disrupt the agricultural supply chain, and push food prices higher, increasing challenges for consumers and agri-businesses. Close monitoring and coordinated response plans, including subsidies and strategic reserves, are required.  
- 🔨 **Rising cost pressures in construction may slow economic growth momentum.** Construction material prices up 30%, along with labor shortages, limit infrastructure project scales, compelling the government to consider unprecedented stimulus measures like home loan interest support to avoid real estate market disruptions.  
- ⚡ **Energy must balance traditional and renewable sources for long-term economic stability.** While Vietnam promotes renewable energy, heavy industry, construction, and transport still rely on oil and gas. Concurrent development of strategic reserves and domestic refining infrastructure is vital.  
- 🌍 **Geopolitical developments and the global energy crisis remain unpredictable factors.** The U.S.-China summit and Middle East crisis distinctly affect global supply chains and oil prices. Vietnam can leverage its intermediary position and multilateral relationships to attract investment and foster sustainable economic development.  
- 💸 **Domestic investors play a crucial role in maintaining Vietnam’s stock market amid strong foreign capital outflows.** Confidence and increased knowledge among domestic individual investors help preserve liquidity and market appeal even during volatile geopolitical and global economic periods.

# Overall Conclusion  
Vietnam stands at a crossroads between adapting to global volatility and developing internal strengths. Ensuring multi-modal energy security along with macroeconomic stability strategies, credit policy innovation, sustainable energy development, and climate change adaptation will determine the economy’s future direction. Although risks remain, positive signals from Vietnam’s market and policies provide a strong foundation for long-term, stable growth.

# 🎤 SPEAKING PRACTICE — Sau khi nghe

> **Cách dùng:** Làm **theo thứ tự 5 level**. Mỗi câu **nói TO, bấm hẹn giờ**, không viết ra giấy trước. Ghi âm lại nếu được.
> Nguyên tắc: Level 1–2 nhìn bài được. **Level 3–5 GẤP BÀI LẠI.**

---

## 🟢 LEVEL 1 — Recall (10–15 giây/câu, nhìn bài được)

Mục tiêu: bật ra được **số liệu + danh từ chuyên ngành**. Trả lời **cả câu**, không trả lời một từ.

1. How many barrels of oil has China stockpiled?
2. Roughly what share of the VN-Index do Vingroup shares account for?
3. How many days of oil reserves does Vietnam have, compared to China?
4. By how much did construction material prices rise?
5. What is Vietnam's inflation rate, and how does it compare to the Philippines?
6. What weather phenomenon poses a risk to Vietnamese agriculture?
7. Are foreign investors currently net buyers or net sellers in Vietnam?

> ⚠️ **Bẫy số liệu**: đọc số bằng tiếng Anh là chỗ người Việt ngập ngừng nhất.
> *2 billion barrels* · *nearly 30%* · *around 5.5%* · *by 30%* · *3–4 degrees Celsius above normal*
> Nói lại từng con số **3 lần** cho trôi trước khi qua Level 2.

---

## 🟡 LEVEL 2 — Paraphrase (30–45 giây/câu, nhìn bài được)

Mục tiêu: **diễn đạt lại bằng từ của mình**, không đọc lại nguyên văn.

8. In your own words, why is Vietnam's economy holding up better than its neighbours right now?
9. Explain what "oil diplomacy" means and why China is using it.
10. Explain the risk of the VN-Index depending on one company. Why is that both a strength and a warning sign?
11. Why does the article argue Vietnam shouldn't focus *only* on renewable energy?
12. Describe the situation in construction — high demand but what's holding it back?

> 💡 Mỗi câu bắt đầu bằng một trong các frame này (đừng nhảy thẳng vào nội dung):
> **"The main point here is that…"** · **"Basically, what's happening is…"** · **"There are two reasons for this. First,…"**

---

## 🟠 LEVEL 3 — Summarize (60 giây, **GẤP BÀI**)

13. **Summarize the whole article in 60 seconds.** Cover: energy, the stock market, El Niño, construction.
14. **Summarize it in 20 seconds.** Chỉ 2 câu — ý chính + rủi ro lớn nhất.
15. Pick the **single most important risk** for Vietnam and explain why you picked that one over the others.

> 🔑 Bộ khung cho câu 13 — học thuộc khung này, đổi nội dung là dùng được cho **mọi** bài summary:
> *"The article covers four main areas. **First**, on the energy side, … **Second**, in terms of the stock market, … **Third**, there's a climate risk — … **And finally**, … **Overall**, the picture is [X], **though** [Y]."*

---

## 🔴 LEVEL 4 — Opinion (60 giây, TOEIC Q11 style, **GẤP BÀI**)

Đây là dạng **chấm điểm TOEIC Speaking Q11**. Bắt buộc: **nêu quan điểm → 2 lý do → ví dụ → chốt lại.**

16. Some people say Vietnam should invest in oil refining and reserves first, and renewable energy later. Others say the opposite. **Which do you agree with, and why?**
17. Do you think it's healthy for a stock market when one company makes up 30% of the index? Why or why not?
18. Should the government subsidise fuel prices, or let the market decide? Give reasons.
19. As someone working in tech — do you think the shift of international investment into **data centres** is a real opportunity for Vietnam? What would need to change first?

> 🔑 Khung 60 giây (đếm nhịp): **10s** quan điểm → **20s** lý do 1 + ví dụ → **20s** lý do 2 → **10s** chốt.
> *"I'd say… / In my view… "* → *"The main reason is that… For example, …"* → *"On top of that, …"* → *"So overall, I'd argue that…"*

---

## 🔵 LEVEL 5 — Workplace transfer (role-play, **GẤP BÀI**)

Dạng sát nhất với công việc thật của bạn — **giải thích bối cảnh Việt Nam cho client phương Tây**.

20. Your Swedish client asks over coffee: *"So, how's the economy doing in Vietnam? We keep reading about the energy crisis."* → Trả lời trong **45 giây**, giọng chuyên nghiệp nhưng tự nhiên.
21. The client is nervous about **cost increases** on a long-term project. Explain the inflation and labour situation **without** making them panic — you need to be honest but reassuring.
22. The client asks: *"Is it risky to open a delivery centre in Hanoi right now?"* → Đưa **2 điểm tích cực và 1 rủi ro**, rồi đưa ra khuyến nghị.

> 💡 Câu 21 là bài khó nhất: bạn phải **nói tin xấu mà vẫn giữ được sự tin tưởng**. Dùng công thức:
> *"To be upfront with you, [tin xấu]. **That said**, [yếu tố giảm nhẹ]. **What I'd suggest is** [hành động]."*

## 📚 Từ vựng BẮT BUỘC dùng lại

Nghe hiểu là **chưa đủ** — phải **nói ra được**. Mục tiêu: dùng ít nhất **8 cụm** dưới đây trong Level 3–5.

### Kinh tế & thị trường
| Cụm | Nghĩa | Nói thử |
|---|---|---|
| **maintain growth momentum** | duy trì đà tăng trưởng | "Vietnam has managed to maintain growth momentum." |
| **volatility / volatile** | sự biến động / dễ biến động | "amid global volatility" |
| **resilience** | sức chống chịu | "the economy has shown real resilience" |
| **net selling / net buyers** | bán ròng / mua ròng | "Foreign investors are still net sellers." |
| **market capitalisation** | vốn hoá thị trường | |
| **diversify (the market base)** | đa dạng hoá | "The market base needs to be diversified." |
| **at a crossroads** | ở ngã ba đường | "Vietnam is at a crossroads." |

### Năng lượng & rủi ro
| Cụm | Nghĩa | Nói thử |
|---|---|---|
| **stockpile (v/n)** | tích trữ | "China has stockpiled 2 billion barrels." |
| **draw down reserves** | rút bớt dự trữ | |
| **reduce dependence on** | giảm phụ thuộc vào | "…to reduce dependence on imports" |
| **ease / curb a crisis** | làm dịu / kìm hãm | "…to ease the global energy crisis" |
| **pose a risk to** | gây rủi ro cho | "El Niño poses a risk to agriculture." |
| **offset** | bù đắp, triệt tiêu | "Domestic demand offset the foreign outflows." |
| **absorption capacity** | khả năng hấp thụ | |

### 🔗 Ba cấu trúc từ file grammar — dùng được ngay trong bài này

> **`It remains to be seen whether…`** → *"It remains to be seen whether El Niño will be as severe as forecast."*
> **`have yet to`** → *"Vietnam has yet to build up meaningful strategic reserves."*
> **`That said, …`** → *"Inflation is under control. That said, food prices are a real concern."*

## ✅ Tự chấm sau khi nói (thang TOEIC)

Sau mỗi câu Level 3–5, tự tick:

- [ ] **Fluency** — nói liền mạch, không dừng quá 3 giây để tìm từ
- [ ] **Structure** — có mở đầu, thân, chốt (không lan man rồi tắt)
- [ ] **Vocabulary** — dùng được ≥2 cụm chuyên ngành ở trên (không nói vòng vo bằng từ đơn giản)
- [ ] **Grammar** — thì nhất quán; không lẫn present perfect với past simple
- [ ] **Pronunciation** — đọc trôi các con số và tên riêng (*El Niño, VN-Index, Vingroup, LNG, SPR*)

> ⚠️ Lỗi hay gặp nhất ở bài này: **lẫn thì**. Số liệu quá khứ (*prices rose 30%*) và tình hình hiện tại (*prices are still high*) trộn vào nhau. Chọn thì trước khi nói.
