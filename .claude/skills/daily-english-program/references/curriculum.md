# 30-Day Curriculum — Intermediate → Advanced

Each day = **Theme** · **Function** (what you do with language) · **Grammar** (the day's one new structure) · **Vocab theme** · **Signature frames** (seed phrases to expand into the full 8-10).

The skill reads the target day here, then expands it into the full 6-part package. Titles here are the source of truth for the progress file. Some days carry a **Secondary** structure — a smaller point taught alongside the primary one so that, together, the 30 days close every gap in the coverage matrix below. Teach the secondary point briefly too (don't skip it).

---

## WEEK 1 — Foundations (B1 → B1+)

### Day 1 — Daily routine & lifestyle
- **Function:** describe habits & a typical day
- **Grammar:** present simple vs present continuous (habit/permanent vs now/temporary) + adverbs of frequency
- **Vocab:** routine verbs (commute, grab a coffee, wind down, run errands), time markers
- **Frames:** "On a typical day, I…" · "I usually / tend to…" · "Right now I'm …ing" · "As a rule, I…" · "The first thing I do is…"

### Day 2 — Home & neighborhood
- **Function:** describe places & location + ask/give directions
- **Grammar:** there is/are + prepositions of place + quantifiers (some/any/much/many)
- **Secondary:** asking & giving directions (imperatives: go straight, turn left, it's opposite…)
- **Vocab:** rooms, furniture, amenities (within walking distance, around the corner)
- **Frames:** "It's within walking distance of…" · "There's a … just around the corner." · "How do I get to…?" · "Go straight and turn left at…" · "It's right opposite the…"

### Day 3 — Food, cooking & eating out
- **Function:** order food, describe dishes, state preferences
- **Grammar:** countable/uncountable + a/an/some/any + "would like"
- **Vocab:** cooking verbs (chop, stir, grill, season), taste adjectives (savoury, rich, bland)
- **Frames:** "I'd like… / Could I get…" · "It's a bit too…" · "It tastes like…" · "I'm not a big fan of…" · "It's to die for."

### Day 4 — A memorable day (telling a story)
- **Function:** narrate past events
- **Grammar:** past simple vs past continuous + time linkers (when/while/as soon as)
- **Vocab:** sequence words, reaction verbs
- **Frames:** "So there I was, …" · "While I was …, suddenly…" · "The next thing I knew…" · "It turned out (that)…"

### Day 5 — Travel & experiences
- **Function:** talk about things you have / haven't done
- **Grammar:** present perfect vs past simple + been/gone + ever/never/yet/already
- **Vocab:** travel nouns, strong adjectives (breathtaking, packed, overrated)
- **Frames:** "I've always wanted to…" · "I've never actually…" · "Have you ever…?" · "One place that really stuck with me was…"

### Day 6 — Shopping, money & choices
- **Function:** compare options & decide
- **Grammar:** comparatives & superlatives + too/enough + not as … as
- **Vocab:** money verbs (afford, splurge, save up, be worth), shopping
- **Frames:** "It's not worth it." · "That's a bit of a rip-off." · "It's way more … than…" · "I'd rather … than…"

### Day 7 — Weekend & free time  ·  *WEEK 1 REVIEW*
- **Function:** talk about leisure + recycle Days 1–6
- **Grammar:** review present/past/perfect + like/love/hate + V-ing
- **Vocab:** hobbies, relaxation
- **Frames:** "I'm really into…" · "I spend most of my free time …ing" · "I can't get enough of…" · "I've been meaning to…"
- **Note:** review day — deliberately mix Week 1 structures in the model speech.

## WEEK 2 — Social & narrative (B1+ → B2)

### Day 8 — Requests, permission, obligation & rules
- **Function:** make/respond to requests, ask permission, state what's required/allowed, complain politely
- **Grammar:** modals of request & permission (can/could/would/may) + indirect questions
- **Secondary:** modals of obligation & prohibition (must / have to / don't have to / needn't / be (not) allowed to)
- **Vocab:** phone & service phrases; rules & policy
- **Frames:** "Would you mind …ing?" · "Could you possibly…?" · "Is it OK if I…?" · "You're not allowed to…" · "You don't have to…, but…" · "Sorry to bother you, but…"

### Day 9 — Hobbies, interests & skills
- **Function:** say what you do and how well
- **Grammar:** gerunds vs infinitives (enjoy doing / want to do / good at doing)
- **Vocab:** skill adjectives, learning verbs
- **Frames:** "I'm getting the hang of…" · "I've picked up…" · "I'm not much of a…" · "I'm rusty at…"

### Day 10 — Health, body & fitness
- **Function:** describe symptoms, habits & duration
- **Grammar:** present perfect continuous (for/since, duration, ongoing effect)
- **Vocab:** body, ailments, fitness
- **Frames:** "I've been feeling…" · "I've had a … for a few days." · "I keep …ing" · "You should probably get it checked."

### Day 11 — Plans, invitations & arrangements
- **Function:** make plans & invite
- **Grammar:** future forms (will vs going to vs present continuous for arrangements)
- **Secondary:** future continuous ("This time tomorrow I'll be …ing") for actions in progress in the future
- **Vocab:** scheduling, socialising
- **Frames:** "Are you free…?" · "Do you fancy …ing?" · "I'm meeting … later." · "Let's say around…" · "I'll be …ing by then."

### Day 12 — Dreams, wishes & imagination
- **Function:** talk about hypothetical present situations
- **Grammar:** second conditional (If I …, I would…)
- **Vocab:** aspiration, possibility
- **Frames:** "If I had the chance, I'd…" · "I'd love to be able to…" · "Imagine if…" · "In an ideal world…"

### Day 13 — Regrets & what I'd change
- **Function:** express regret about the past
- **Grammar:** third conditional + wish/if only + past perfect
- **Vocab:** regret, hindsight
- **Frames:** "I wish I'd…" · "If only I had…" · "Looking back, I should have…" · "I regret not …ing"

### Day 14 — Retelling what people said  ·  *WEEK 2 REVIEW*
- **Function:** report conversations & news
- **Grammar:** reported speech (tense backshift; say/tell/ask)
- **Vocab:** reporting verbs (mention, claim, admit, point out, insist)
- **Frames:** "She told me (that)…" · "He was saying…" · "Apparently, …" · "Word is that…"
- **Note:** review day — weave conditionals + narration from Week 2 into the speech.

## WEEK 3 — Explaining & reasoning (B2)

### Day 15 — How things work / processes
- **Function:** explain processes & how things are done
- **Grammar:** passive voice (present & past) + sequence linkers
- **Vocab:** process verbs (assemble, process, deliver, generate)
- **Frames:** "First, … is done." · "It's made of / made from…" · "Once that's finished, …" · "The … gets … and then…"

### Day 16 — Describing people & places in detail
- **Function:** rich, layered description
- **Grammar:** relative clauses (defining & non-defining; who/which/where/whose)
- **Vocab:** personality & appearance, atmosphere adjectives
- **Frames:** "He's the kind of person who…" · "It's a place where…" · "…, which is exactly why…" · "The thing that stands out is…"

### Day 17 — Guessing about everyday situations
- **Function:** make guesses & draw conclusions from clues (whose is this? why is he late? what happened?)
- **Grammar:** modals of deduction (must/might/could/can't + be / have been)
- **Vocab:** probability words (probably, maybe, definitely, no way)
- **Frames:** "He must be stuck in traffic." · "That can't be right." · "It might be…" · "I bet…" · "There's a good chance…"

### Day 18 — Childhood & how things used to be
- **Function:** past habits & how things changed
- **Grammar:** used to / would (past habit) + be/get used to
- **Vocab:** childhood, change over time
- **Frames:** "I used to…" · "Back then, we'd…" · "I'm still getting used to…" · "Things aren't what they used to be."

### Day 19 — Weather & everyday plans
- **Function:** talk about weather & how it changes your plans (everyday cause → result)
- **Grammar:** zero & first conditional (If it rains, I take a taxi / If it's sunny, I'll…)
- **Vocab:** weather, seasons, everyday reactions to weather
- **Frames:** "If it rains, I usually…" · "Whenever it's hot, …" · "If the weather's nice, I'll…" · "It looks like it's going to…"

### Day 20 — Tech & gadgets in everyday life
- **Function:** describe usage + pros/cons
- **Grammar:** phrasal verbs (systematic: separable vs inseparable) + present perfect for recent events
- **Vocab:** tech phrasal verbs (set up, back up, log in, run out, plug in, crash)
- **Frames:** "It keeps …ing on me." · "I can't get it to…" · "You just set it up and…" · "It's acting up again."

### Day 21 — Problems, cause–effect & solutions  ·  *WEEK 3 REVIEW*
- **Function:** state a problem, explain its cause/effect, propose a fix
- **Grammar:** cause–effect, result & purpose linkers (because / since / due to / so (that) / therefore / as a result / to + V / in order to)
- **Secondary:** review passive + conditionals + relatives from Week 3
- **Vocab:** problem/solution language
- **Frames:** "The issue is (that)…" · "It's mainly due to…" · "As a result, …" · "What we could do is…" · "…so that we don't…" · "That should sort it out."
- **Note:** review day — integrate Week 3 structures around the cause–effect spine.

## WEEK 4 — Nuance & fluency (B2 → C1)

### Day 22 — Opinions with nuance (agree / disagree)
- **Function:** give balanced, diplomatic opinions
- **Grammar:** concession (although/though/even though/despite/whereas) + contrast linkers (however/on the other hand)
- **Secondary:** question tags for seeking agreement/confirming ("…, isn't it?" · "…, don't you think?" · "…, right?")
- **Vocab:** opinion & stance language
- **Frames:** "I see your point, but…" · "That said, …" · "While I agree that…, I still think…" · "It's a great idea, isn't it?" · "It depends on…"

### Day 23 — My life & job: then vs now
- **Function:** compare your own past vs present (routine, job, city) over time
- **Grammar:** comparatives with intensifiers (far / slightly / nowhere near / a bit) + used-to-vs-now
- **Vocab:** change in everyday life & work
- **Frames:** "Compared to a few years ago, …" · "These days I…, whereas before I…" · "My job is far more … now." · "It's nowhere near as … as it used to be."

### Day 24 — What I like (and don't like) about my life & work
- **Function:** emphasise what you value/enjoy or dislike day to day
- **Grammar:** cleft sentences (What I love about my job is… / The thing I like most is… / It's X that…)
- **Vocab:** likes/dislikes, work & life preferences
- **Frames:** "What I really like about … is…" · "The one thing I can't stand is…" · "It's not the … that bothers me, it's…" · "All I want is…"

### Day 25 — Telling a story from work or life with impact
- **Function:** narrate a real personal story (a surprise, a proud moment, a funny mishap) with emphasis
- **Grammar:** inversion for emphasis (Not only…, Never have I…, Little did I know…) — used as an occasional flourish, not every sentence
- **Vocab:** surprise, pride, reactions
- **Frames:** "Not only did I…, but I also…" · "Never have I felt so…" · "Little did I know…" · "Only later did I realise…"

### Day 26 — Giving advice diplomatically
- **Function:** advise & suggest without imposing
- **Grammar:** hedging & softening (tend to, might want to, I'd say, sort of, a bit) + should/ought/could
- **Vocab:** advice, suggestion
- **Frames:** "You might want to…" · "If I were you, I'd…" · "Have you thought about…?" · "It might be worth …ing"

### Day 27 — Hopes, wishes & 'if only' (nuance)
- **Function:** complex wishes about present & past
- **Grammar:** wish + past / past perfect / would + mixed conditionals
- **Vocab:** emotion, longing
- **Frames:** "I wish things were…" · "If only I could…" · "I wish you wouldn't…" · "Had I known, I'd have…"

### Day 28 — Negotiating & setting conditions  ·  *LATE REVIEW*
- **Function:** bargain & set terms
- **Grammar:** conditionals with unless / provided (that) / as long as / in case
- **Vocab:** negotiation, compromise
- **Frames:** "I'd be happy to … as long as…" · "Provided that…" · "Unless…, we won't be able to…" · "How about we meet in the middle?"
- **Note:** review day — consolidate the whole conditional family.

### Day 29 — Keeping a conversation alive (react · clarify · small talk)
- **Function:** manage real conversation — react, buy time, check/repair understanding, interrupt politely, use common idioms
- **Grammar:** fixed conversational chunks (fluency over rules)
- **Vocab:** reactions (No way! / That's a shame / Good for you), fillers (well, you know, I mean), repair (Sorry, could you say that again?), a few everyday idioms (a piece of cake, on the same page, touch base)
- **Frames:** "Sorry, what do you mean by…?" · "Let me put it another way…" · "Hang on, so you're saying…?" · "Anyway, …" · "We're on the same page." · "At the end of the day…"

### Day 30 — Free topic / spontaneous fluency  ·  *CAPSTONE*
- **Function:** speak unscripted for ~2 minutes on any given topic
- **Grammar:** integrate everything; self-monitor & self-correct
- **Vocab:** recycle the strongest sets from the month
- **Frames:** improvise from all previous days
- **Note:** capstone — give the user a random topic (or let them pick), have them deliver unscripted, then hand to `workplace-english-coach` Mode 5 for a **full month-in-review debrief** (patterns fixed, patterns still to watch).
