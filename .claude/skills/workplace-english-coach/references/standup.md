# Daily Standup

The shortest English of the day, but high-frequency reps. Lowest priority for this user, but worth getting smooth because you do it every day.

## The classic structure

1. **Yesterday** — what you completed (not what you worked on — what you finished)
2. **Today** — what you'll move forward
3. **Blockers** — what's stopping you, or "no blockers"

## Stock phrases

### Opening
- **"Hey team — quick one from me."**
- **"Morning. Yesterday I…"** — just dive in, no preamble

### Yesterday
- **"Yesterday I wrapped up [X] and started on [Y]."**
- **"Got [X] merged."** — past tense, specific
- **"Spent most of yesterday investigating [bug]. Found the root cause — it's [thing]."**
- **"Pushed [X] over the line."** — idiom = finished something that had been dragging

### Today
- **"Today I'm picking up [Y]."**
- **"Heads-down on [X] today."** — focused work
- **"Pairing with [name] on [Z] this morning."**
- **"Trying to land [X] before EOD."** — "land" = finish and ship

### Blockers
- **"No blockers."** — clean and fine
- **"One blocker: waiting on [thing] from [team/person]."** — specific
- **"Bit stuck on [problem] — could use 15 mins with someone after standup."** — actionable ask
- **"Yellow flag on [X] — not blocked yet but might be by Wednesday if [thing] doesn't move."** — proactive flagging

### When you didn't get much done yesterday (happens)
- **"Got pulled into [unexpected thing] most of yesterday, so [planned task] didn't progress. Picking it up first thing today."**
- **"Lighter day yesterday — was in interviews / sick / etc."** — fine to be honest

### Async standup (Slack message format)
> **Yesterday**: shipped X, reviewed Y's PR, debugged Z
> **Today**: continue on [feature], pair with [name] on [thing]
> **Blockers**: none / waiting on [X]

## Tone notes

- **Don't apologize** for normal pace. "Sorry I didn't finish X" should be reserved for actual misses against commitments.
- **Brevity is professional** in standup. Long status = unclear thinking.
- **Specific verbs** beat generic ones: "shipped", "merged", "spiked", "drafted", "reviewed", "deployed", "rolled back", "rolled out" — much better than "worked on".

## Common Vietnamese-speaker patterns to fix

- ❌ "I working on X" → ✅ "I'm working on X" / "I'm wrapping up X"
- ❌ "Yesterday I worked on..." (vague) → ✅ "Yesterday I shipped..." / "Yesterday I finished..."
- ❌ "Today I will continue with X" → ✅ "Today I'm picking up X" (more natural, present continuous)
- ❌ "I'm blocked because…" (long explanation) → ✅ "Blocker: [one phrase]. Need [specific thing]."
