# TOEIC Writing — Prompt Bank

Exam-style prompts for drills and full simulations. Use directly or generate new ones in the same style.

## Q1–5 Write a sentence based on a picture
Format: give the **2 required words** + a one-line picture description. The user writes ONE sentence using both words.

1. Words: **wait / because** — *Picture: people standing in a line at a bus stop in the rain.*
2. Words: **repair / while** — *Picture: a mechanic under a car; another worker hands him a tool.*
3. Words: **next to / read** — *Picture: a woman on a sofa reading a book; a cat sits beside her.*
4. Words: **carry / heavy** — *Picture: a delivery man lifting a large box out of a van.*
5. Words: **point / map** — *Picture: two tourists looking at a map; one points at a street.*
6. Words: **although / busy** — *Picture: a crowded restaurant with a waiter taking an order.*
7. Words: **present / meeting** — *Picture: a man standing at a screen showing charts to seated colleagues.*
8. Words: **water / garden** — *Picture: an older man using a hose on flowers in a backyard.*

## Q6–7 Respond to a written request (email)
Format: give the email + the instruction line (the required actions). 10 minutes each.

**Email 1 — Customer enquiry**
> From: A new customer
> Subject: Question about your software
> "Hi, I'm considering your project-management tool for my small team of five. Before I sign up, I have a few concerns. Thank you."
>
> **Directions:** Respond as a sales representative. In your email, GIVE one piece of information about the product, ASK two questions about the customer's needs, and MAKE one suggestion.

**Email 2 — From your manager**
> From: Your manager
> Subject: Client meeting on Friday
> "Could you prepare a short status update for Friday's client meeting? Let me know if you have any blockers."
>
> **Directions:** Reply to your manager. CONFIRM you can prepare the update, ASK one question about what to include, and REQUEST one thing you need to finish it.

**Email 3 — Scheduling problem**
> From: A colleague
> Subject: Rescheduling our review
> "Something came up and I can't make our 2 p.m. code review tomorrow. Can we find another time?"
>
> **Directions:** Reply to your colleague. APOLOGIZE is not needed, but PROPOSE two alternative times, ASK one question, and STATE one thing you'll prepare beforehand.

**Email 4 — Complaint**
> From: A customer
> Subject: Late delivery
> "My order #4521 was supposed to arrive last week but I still haven't received it. This is very disappointing."
>
> **Directions:** Respond as customer support. APOLOGIZE, GIVE one piece of information about the delay, and OFFER one solution.

## Q8 Opinion essay (~300 words, 30 min)

1. Do you agree or disagree with the following statement? *"Working from home is better than working in a traditional office."* Give reasons and specific examples.
2. Some people think it is better to spend money on experiences (like travel) than on possessions. What is your opinion? Support it with reasons and examples.
3. Do you agree that companies should require all employees to learn new technology skills regularly? Why or why not?
4. Some believe that social media does more harm than good. Do you agree or disagree? Explain with examples.
5. Do you think large cities or small towns are better places to raise a family? State your opinion and support it.
6. Do you agree with the statement that *"it is more important to enjoy your job than to earn a high salary"*? Give reasons.
7. Some people prefer to make decisions quickly; others like to take their time. Which approach do you think is better, and why?
8. Do you agree that schools should teach students how to use artificial intelligence tools? Support your opinion with specific reasons.
