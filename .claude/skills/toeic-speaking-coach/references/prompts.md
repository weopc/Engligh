# TOEIC Speaking — Prompt Bank

Exam-style prompts for drills and full simulations. Use these directly or generate new ones in the same style. Default examples toward the user's world (tech/office/Hanoi) when it fits, but keep general topics too — the real test is broad.

## Q1–2 Read a text aloud (give the text; 45s prep / 45s read)

1. *(Announcement)* "Attention, passengers. The 9:15 train to the city center has been delayed by approximately twenty minutes due to a signaling problem. We apologize for the inconvenience and recommend that travelers with connecting services check the departure boards for updated platform information."
2. *(Advertisement)* "Looking for a faster way to manage your team's projects? Our new software lets you assign tasks, track progress, and share files in one place. Sign up today for a free thirty-day trial, and discover why thousands of growing companies trust us to keep their work organized."
3. *(Voicemail/business)* "Hello, this is a message for Ms. Tran. I'm calling to confirm your appointment on Thursday afternoon. If that time still works for you, there's no need to call back. However, if you'd like to reschedule, please contact our office before five o'clock on Wednesday. Thank you, and have a great day."
4. *(News)* "Local officials announced today that the new public library will open next month. The modern building features a children's reading room, several quiet study areas, and free high-speed internet. Residents are encouraged to attend the grand opening, which will include guided tours, live music, and refreshments for the whole family."

## Q3–4 Describe a picture (describe the scene in words; user describes it back; 45s prep / 30s response)

1. *An open-plan office. In the foreground, a young woman in a blue shirt is sitting at a desk, typing on a laptop. Behind her, two colleagues are standing near a whiteboard covered in sticky notes, appearing to discuss something. There are coffee cups and papers on the desks, and large windows on the right let in daylight.*
2. *A busy coffee shop. A barista in an apron is handing a paper cup to a customer at the counter. Several people are sitting at wooden tables, some looking at their phones, one reading a book. A chalkboard menu hangs on the wall behind the counter.*
3. *An outdoor market. Many stalls are selling fresh fruit and vegetables. In the center, an older man wearing a hat is weighing produce on a scale while a woman points at some apples. The ground is crowded with shoppers carrying bags.*
4. *A team meeting in a conference room. Five people are seated around a long table; one man is standing and presenting slides on a screen. The others are looking toward the screen, and a few are taking notes on laptops.*

## Q5–7 Respond to questions (no reading; pick a topic set; 3s prep)

**Set A — Technology habits**
- Q5 (15s): How often do you use your smartphone during a typical day?
- Q6 (15s): What is your favorite app, and what do you use it for?
- Q7 (30s): Some people say we rely too much on technology. What do you think, and why?

**Set B — Work & routine**
- Q5 (15s): What time do you usually start work?
- Q6 (15s): How do you get to work each day?
- Q7 (30s): If a friend were starting your job, what advice would you give them about a typical day?

**Set C — Travel**
- Q5 (15s): Where did you go on your last trip?
- Q6 (15s): Do you prefer traveling alone or with others?
- Q7 (30s): What's one place you'd recommend a visitor see in your city, and why?

## Q8–10 Respond using information provided (give the agenda, then the 3 questions; 45s read)

- Q8 (15s): What time does the opening keynote start, and where is it held?
- Q9 (15s): I heard there's a workshop about AI in the afternoon — is that right? Who is leading it?
- Q10 (30s): I'm interested in the morning sessions. Could you tell me what's happening between 10 a.m. and noon?

## Q11 Express an opinion (45s prep / 60s response)

1. Do you prefer working from home or working in an office? State your opinion and give reasons.
2. Some people think companies should require employees to learn new skills every year. Do you agree or disagree? Why?
3. Do you think it is better for students to study alone or in groups? Explain your choice.
4. Some people believe technology has made our lives more stressful, not easier. What is your opinion?
5. Is it better to be an expert in one field or to have skills in many areas? Give reasons and examples.
6. Should children be allowed to use smartphones before the age of twelve? State and support your view.
