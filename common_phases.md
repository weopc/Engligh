# Phrasal Verbs — Luyện NÓI (Công sở + TOEIC)

> **Cách dùng:** đọc cụm + nghĩa → **nói TO 2–3 câu mẫu** không nhìn → lặp đến khi bật ra tự động. Mỗi ngày 5 cụm. Mục tiêu: nói không cần nghĩ.

### 📌 Ký hiệu tách được / không tách (QUAN TRỌNG NHẤT)
- `[tách]` = **separable**: tân ngữ chen được vào giữa. Nếu tân ngữ là **đại từ** (it / them / you) thì **BẮT BUỘC** để giữa.
  - ✅ *set it up* / *turn it off* — ❌ *set up it* / *turn off it*
  - Danh từ thì để đâu cũng được: *set up the server* = *set the server up*.
- `[không tách]` = **inseparable**: tân ngữ luôn đứng **sau** cả cụm.
  - ✅ *look into it* / *deal with it* — ❌ *look it into*
- `[tự thân]` = **intransitive**: không có tân ngữ trực tiếp (thường đi kèm *with / on*).

---

## NHÓM 1 — Việc & Task

### set up = thiết lập / sắp xếp `[tách]`
- Let me set up a meeting for tomorrow.
- I'll set up the test environment this afternoon.
- Can you set it up by Friday?
> ⚠️ ĐỘNG TỪ = **set up** (2 chữ) | DANH TỪ = **a setup** (1 chữ). Đại từ phải để giữa: *set it up* ✓ / *set up it* ✗

### follow up = theo dõi tiếp / liên hệ lại `[tự thân + on/with]`
- I'll follow up with the client tomorrow.
- Let me follow up on that email.
- Can you follow up with the QA team?
> ⚠️ liên hệ **với người** → *follow up **with** [người]* | theo dõi **việc** → *follow up **on** [việc]*

### figure out = tìm ra / hiểu ra `[tách]`
- I need to figure out what's causing the bug.
- Let me figure it out and get back to you.
- We finally figured it out.
> ⚠️ *figure it out* ✓ / *figure out it* ✗

### come up with = nghĩ ra `[không tách — cụm 3 chữ]`
- We need to come up with a better solution.
- I came up with a few ideas over the weekend.
- Can you come up with an estimate by tomorrow?
> ⚠️ cụm 3 phần không tách: *come up with it* ✓

### look into = điều tra / tìm hiểu `[không tách]`
- I'll look into the issue right away.
- Let me look into it and let you know.
- We're still looking into the root cause.

---

## NHÓM 2 — Thời gian & Lịch

### push back = hoãn lại (lùi thời gian) `[tách]`
- Let's push the deadline back to Friday.
- They pushed back the launch by a week.
- Can we push it back an hour?
> ⚠️ Nghĩa khác: *push back **on** [ý kiến]* = phản đối. Ở đây là **lùi lịch**.

### call off = hủy `[tách]`
- We had to call off the meeting.
- They called off the release because of a bug.
- The event was called off due to bad weather.

### run out of = hết (cái gì) `[không tách]`
- We're running out of time.
- I've run out of ideas on this one.
- We ran out of budget last quarter.

### move up = dời lên sớm hơn `[tách]`
- Can we move up the deadline?
- They moved the launch date up by two weeks.
- Let's move it up to 9 AM.
> ⚠️ Ngược với *push back*: *move up* = sớm hơn, *push back* = trễ hơn.

### wrap up = kết thúc `[tách]`
- Let's wrap up the meeting.
- I'm trying to wrap up this task before lunch.
- Let's wrap it up — we're out of time.

---

## NHÓM 3 — Giao tiếp & Họp

### bring up = nêu ra (vấn đề) `[tách]`
- I'd like to bring up a concern.
- She brought up a good point in the meeting.
- Let's bring it up with the team.

### point out = chỉ ra `[tách]`
- He pointed out a mistake in my code.
- Thanks for pointing that out.
- Let me point out a few things first.

### go over = xem lại / rà soát `[không tách]`
- Let's go over the report together.
- Can you go over the contract before we send it?
- I'll go over it with you later.

### get back to = trả lời sau `[không tách]`
- Let me check and get back to you.
- I'll get back to you by EOD.
- Can you get back to me on this tomorrow?
> ⚠️ EOD = *end of day* (cuối ngày làm việc).

### fill in (someone) = báo cho ai biết `[tách]`
- Let me fill you in on what happened.
- Can you fill me in on the meeting?
- I'll fill the team in later.
> ⚠️ *fill **in** [người] on [việc]*. Đừng nhầm với *fill **out** a form* = điền đơn.

---

## NHÓM 4 — Vấn đề & Giải quyết

### deal with = xử lý `[không tách]`
- I'll deal with the client's complaint.
- We need to deal with this issue first.
- Let me deal with it.

### break down = hỏng / sập `[tự thân]`
- The server broke down last night.
- The system keeps breaking down.
- If it breaks down again, call me.
> ⚠️ DANH TỪ *a breakdown* = sự cố / bảng phân tích. ĐT không tách.

### hold up = làm chậm trễ `[tách]`
- What's holding up the release?
- The bug is holding up the whole project.
- Don't let this hold you up.

### come across = tình cờ gặp / bắt gặp `[không tách]`
- I came across a strange bug today.
- I came across a useful tool last week.
- If you come across any issues, let me know.

### rule out = loại trừ `[tách]`
- We've ruled out a network problem.
- Let's not rule that out yet.
- We can rule it out.

---

## NHÓM 5 — Triển khai & Hệ thống

### roll out = triển khai `[tách]`
- We'll roll out the feature next week.
- They rolled out the update to all users.
- Let's roll it out gradually.
> ⚠️ DANH TỪ *a rollout* = đợt triển khai.

### back up = sao lưu `[tách]`
- Back up the data before the upgrade.
- Did you back it up?
- We back up everything daily.
> ⚠️ DANH TỪ *a backup* (1 chữ). Nghĩa khác: *back you up* = hậu thuẫn ai đó.

### take over = tiếp quản `[tách]`
- Can you take over this task?
- I'll take over from here.
- She took it over last month.
> ⚠️ DANH TỪ *a takeover* = vụ tiếp quản/thâu tóm.

### hand in / turn in = nộp `[tách]`
- Please hand in the form by Friday.
- I handed it in this morning.
- Don't forget to turn in your report.

### carry out = thực hiện `[tách]`
- We'll carry out the test tomorrow.
- They carried out the plan successfully.
- Let's carry it out step by step.

---

## NHÓM 6 — Đời thường (xã giao công sở)

### catch up = cập nhật / gặp hỏi thăm `[tự thân + on/with]`
- Let's catch up over coffee.
- I need to catch up on emails.
- We should catch up soon.
> ⚠️ *catch up **on** [việc tồn đọng]* | *catch up **with** [người]*.

### get along (with) = hợp nhau `[không tách]`
- I get along with my team.
- They get along really well.
- Do you get along with the new guy?

### look forward to = mong chờ `[không tách — to + V-ing!]`
- I look forward to working with you.
- I'm looking forward to the weekend.
- We look forward to hearing from you.
> ⚠️ *to* ở đây là giới từ → theo sau là **V-ing** (working, hearing), KHÔNG phải nguyên mẫu.

### drop by / stop by = ghé qua `[tự thân]`
- Drop by my desk anytime.
- I'll stop by your office later.
- Feel free to drop by.

### sort out = xử lý ổn thỏa `[tách]`
- Let me sort this out.
- We'll sort it out tomorrow.
- I need to sort out a few things first.

## NHÓM 7 — Bonus (hay gặp trong TOEIC & công sở)

### turn down = từ chối / vặn nhỏ `[tách]`
- They turned down our proposal.
- Can you turn it down a bit? (âm lượng)

### put off = trì hoãn `[tách]`
- Let's not put it off any longer.
- The meeting was put off until next week.

### reach out to = chủ động liên hệ `[không tách]`
- I'll reach out to the vendor today.
- Feel free to reach out to me anytime.

### sign off on = phê duyệt / duyệt cuối `[không tách]`
- The client needs to sign off on the design.
- Once you sign off on it, we'll deploy.

### go through = xem kỹ / trải qua `[không tách]`
- Let's go through the requirements one by one.
- We went through a tough sprint.

### keep up with = theo kịp `[không tách]`
- It's hard to keep up with all the changes.
- Please keep up with the latest updates.

### hand over = bàn giao `[tách]`
- I'll hand over the project before I leave.
- Let me hand it over to the new lead.

### touch base = liên lạc/trao đổi nhanh `[idiom, tự thân]`
- Let's touch base early next week.
- I just wanted to touch base on the timeline.

---

## ⚠️ 4 BẪY hay gặp (nhớ để tránh sai)

1. **Đại từ phải chen vào giữa với `[tách]`**: *turn it off* ✓ / *turn off it* ✗ — *pick him up* ✓ / *pick up him* ✗.
2. **`look forward to` + V-ing**, không phải nguyên mẫu: *look forward to seeing you* ✓ / *to see you* ✗.
3. **Động từ 2 chữ ≠ danh từ 1 chữ**: *set up* (ĐT) / *a setup* (DT); *back up* / *a backup*; *roll out* / *a rollout*.
4. **Cùng cụm, khác nghĩa theo giới từ**: *push it **back*** (lùi lịch) ≠ *push **back on** an idea* (phản đối); *follow up **with** [người]* ≠ *follow up **on** [việc]*.
