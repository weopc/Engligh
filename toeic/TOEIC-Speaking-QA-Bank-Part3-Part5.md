**Dành cho:** tự luyện phản xạ — đọc câu hỏi, tự trả lời trước, rồi mới xem đáp án mẫu.
**Đi kèm:** `toeic/TOEIC-Speaking-Guide.md` (chiến thuật từng dạng) · gõ `/toeic-speaking-coach` để được chấm.

# 🧩 PHẦN CHUNG — "Kho chuyện" tái sử dụng

Đây là kỹ thuật ăn điểm lớn nhất của Part 3 và Part 5: **không nghĩ ví dụ mới cho từng câu.** Chuẩn bị sẵn 6 câu chuyện thật của bạn, rồi nhét vào bất kỳ câu hỏi nào.

| # | Chuyện | Dùng được cho chủ đề |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | Bạn là **tech lead**, dẫn team 6 người, làm dự án cho khách nước ngoài | công việc, giao tiếp, lãnh đạo, kỹ năng, áp lực |
| **2** | Làm **từ xa 2 ngày/tuần**, họp online với khách khác múi giờ | công nghệ, WFH, cân bằng cuộc sống |
| **3** | **Tự học** một công nghệ mới qua khoá online buổi tối | học tập, kỹ năng, tự học vs lớp học |
| **4** | Đi làm bằng **xe máy ở Hà Nội**, ~30 phút, tắc đường | giao thông, thành phố, stress |
| **5** | Cuối tuần **cà phê / gặp bạn / đi bộ quanh Hồ Tây** | giải trí, sức khoẻ, bạn bè |
| **6** | **Chuyến đi Đà Nẵng** gần nhất với gia đình | du lịch, nghỉ ngơi, tiền bạc |

**Cách nhét vào câu trả lời:**
`"For example, in my job as a team lead, ..."` · `"In my own experience, when I was learning ..., I ..."` · `"Last month, my team ..."`

> 💡 Không có ý kiến thật cũng không sao. **Bịa thoải mái.** Giám khảo chấm tiếng Anh, không kiểm chứng đời bạn.

# 🎙️ PART 3 — Q5–7 Respond to questions

### Luật chơi

| | Chuẩn bị | Trả lời | Cần gì |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Q5** | 3 giây | **15s** | 2–3 câu (~30–40 từ) |
| **Q6** | 3 giây | **15s** | 2–3 câu (~30–40 từ) |
| **Q7** | 3 giây | **30s** | 4–6 câu (~65–85 từ) |

**Công thức Q5–6 — A + D (Answer + Detail):** trả lời thẳng → thêm 1 chi tiết hoặc lý do. **Không bao giờ** dừng ở "Yes" / "About four hours".

**Công thức Q7 — P–R–E–C:** Position → Reason → Example → Conclusion.

## Set 1 — Technology habits

*"A marketing firm is doing research in your country. You have agreed to participate in a telephone interview about technology."*

**Q5 (15s):** How often do you use your smartphone during a typical day?
> **Model:** "I use my phone a lot — probably four or five hours a day. I check messages first thing in the morning, and during work I use it mainly for team chats and quick calls."

**Q6 (15s):** What is your favorite app, and what do you use it for?
> **Model:** "My favorite app is Slack, because I use it every single day to talk to my team. It helps me keep track of what everyone is working on without sending long emails."

**Q7 (30s):** Some people say we rely too much on technology. What do you think, and why?
> **Model:** "I partly agree with that. On the one hand, technology makes work much easier — for example, I can work with clients in other countries without travelling at all. On the other hand, a lot of people, including me, find it hard to stop checking their phones, and that really hurts our focus and our sleep. So I'd say the problem isn't technology itself, but how we manage it."

## 🔁 Bộ khung tái dùng cho Part 3

| Tình huống | Câu mở |
|---|---|
| Trả lời tần suất | *"I'd say about … times a week."* · *"Not very often — maybe once a month."* |
| Trả lời sở thích | *"My favorite … is …, mainly because …"* |
| Cần thời gian nghĩ | *"Well, let me think… I'd say…"* · *"That's a good question. Personally, …"* |
| Nêu quan điểm (Q7) | *"I'd say …"* · *"Personally, I think …"* · *"I partly agree with that."* |
| Thêm lý do | *"The main reason is …"* · *"Another reason is …"* · *"On top of that, …"* |
| Nêu ví dụ | *"For example, …"* · *"For instance, …"* · *"In my own experience, …"* |
| Nêu mặt trái | *"That said, …"* · *"On the other hand, …"* |
| Kết | *"So overall, I'd say …"* · *"That's why I think …"* |

### Bẫy của người Việt ở Part 3
- **Trả lời một từ** ("Yes." / "Four hours.") → luôn thêm lý do hoặc chi tiết.
- **Im lặng nghĩ** → dùng filler, đừng để trống.
- **Nhảy thì** giữa câu ("I go… yesterday I used… I will…") → chọn một thì và giữ.
- **"Although…, but…"** → tiếng Anh bỏ "but".
- **"I very like"** → *"I really like"*.

# 💭 PART 5 — Q11 Express an opinion

### Luật chơi

| | |
|---|---|
| **Chuẩn bị** | 45 giây |
| **Trả lời** | **60 giây** |
| **Điểm** | **0–5** (câu nặng nhất cả bài) |
| **Cần gì** | 130–170 từ · 6–9 câu |

### Công thức 4 khối — bám chặt, đừng sáng tạo

| Khối | Thời lượng | Nội dung |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Position** | ~8s | Nêu lập trường ngay câu đầu. *"In my opinion, …"* |
| **2. Reason 1 + Example** | ~20s | *"The main reason is … For example, …"* |
| **3. Reason 2 + Example** | ~20s | *"Another reason is … In my experience, …"* |
| **4. Restate** | ~10s | *"That's why I believe …"* |

### Dùng 45 giây chuẩn bị thế nào
1. **5s** — chọn phe. Chọn phe **dễ nói**, không phải phe bạn tin.
2. **20s** — nghĩ 2 lý do. Dùng bộ lý do vạn năng: **time · money · health · stress · flexibility · quality · relationships · career**.
3. **15s** — gắn 1 ví dụ thật vào mỗi lý do (lấy từ "kho chuyện" ở đầu file).
4. **5s** — nhẩm câu mở đầu để không vấp.

> 🚨 **Lỗi giết điểm số 1:** không nêu rõ lập trường, hoặc đổi phe giữa chừng. Câu đầu tiên phải chốt phe, và giữ đến hết.

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 1: Working from home vs. office

**Prompt:** *Do you prefer working from home or working in an office? State your opinion and give reasons.*

> **Model (≈155 từ):**
> "In my opinion, working from home is more effective for me, although I still value some office time.
> The main reason is focus. At home I have far fewer interruptions, so I can finish difficult technical work much faster. For example, as a team lead I often need to review code, and that requires long quiet blocks. In a busy office, someone taps my shoulder every fifteen minutes and I lose my concentration completely.
> Another reason is the time I save on commuting. My ride to the office takes about thirty minutes each way, so working from home gives me an extra hour every day. I usually use that time to rest or to plan the next day, and it genuinely makes me less stressed.
> That said, I still go to the office for important meetings and for team building, because those things are harder online.
> So overall, I'd choose a flexible mix, but if I had to pick just one, I'd choose working from home."

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 2: Companies requiring yearly training

**Prompt:** *Some people think companies should require employees to learn new skills every year. Do you agree or disagree? Why?*

> **Model (≈150 từ):**
> "I strongly agree that companies should require employees to learn new skills every year.
> The main reason is that industries change extremely quickly, especially in technology. If a company doesn't invest in training, its people fall behind and the business loses to competitors. For instance, in my own field, the tools we used five years ago are almost completely different from what we use today, and my team had to learn several new technologies just to stay competitive.
> Another reason is motivation. People get bored doing exactly the same work for years, and boredom is one of the biggest reasons good employees leave. In my experience, when my company paid for an online course for me, I felt the company cared about my future, so I wanted to stay longer.
> Of course, the training has to be useful and not just a box to tick.
> That's why I believe yearly learning benefits both the company and the employee."

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 3: Studying alone vs. in groups

**Prompt:** *Do you think it is better for students to study alone or in groups? Explain your choice.*

> **Model (≈150 từ):**
> "In my opinion, studying alone is better for most serious learning, though groups have their place.
> The main reason is concentration. When you study alone, you go at your own speed and you can spend extra time on whatever you personally find difficult. For example, when I was preparing for a technical certification, I studied alone in the evenings, and I could repeat the hard chapters as many times as I needed without slowing anyone down.
> Another reason is honesty with yourself. In a group, it's very easy to feel like you understand something just because someone else explained it well, and then you fail when you try it alone.
> That said, groups are excellent for one specific thing: discussion and practice. Explaining an idea to someone else really shows whether you actually understand it.
> So I'd say study alone to learn, then meet a group to test what you've learned."

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 4: Has technology made life more stressful?

**Prompt:** *Some people believe technology has made our lives more stressful, not easier. What is your opinion?*

> **Model (≈155 từ):**
> "I partly agree, but overall I believe technology has made our lives easier rather than more stressful.
> The main reason is the amount of time it saves. Tasks that used to take hours now take minutes — I can pay bills, book a doctor's appointment and hold a meeting with a client in another country, all from my phone, without leaving my desk.
> However, I do understand the other side, and this is where the stress comes from: technology has removed the line between work and personal life. For example, in my job I get messages on my phone in the evening and at weekends, and it's genuinely hard not to answer them. My parents' generation simply left the office and the day was over.
> So my conclusion is that technology itself is not the problem — the problem is that we haven't learned to set limits.
> That's why I think the solution is better habits, not less technology."

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 5: Specialist vs. generalist

**Prompt:** *Is it better to be an expert in one field or to have skills in many areas? Give reasons and examples.*

> **Model (≈150 từ):**
> "In my opinion, it's better to be deep in one field first, and then add skills in other areas.
> The main reason is that real expertise is what companies actually pay for. Anyone can learn the basics of many things, but solving the hardest problems requires deep knowledge, and those people are rare and valuable. For example, in my company, the engineers who are promoted fastest are the ones who are genuinely excellent at one thing.
> However, my second point is that pure specialists hit a ceiling. As a team lead, I found that my technical skill alone wasn't enough — I also needed communication, planning and basic business knowledge to work with clients.
> So the best combination is what people call a T-shape: one deep skill, plus a broad understanding of everything around it.
> That's why I'd tell someone to go deep first, then go wide."

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 6: Smartphones for young children

**Prompt:** *Should children be allowed to use smartphones before the age of twelve? State and support your view.*

> **Model (≈150 từ):**
> "In my opinion, children under twelve should be allowed to use smartphones, but only with clear limits set by their parents.
> The main reason is safety. A phone lets parents contact their child immediately if something happens, and it lets the child call home if they're lost or late. For a family in a big, crowded city like mine, that peace of mind is genuinely valuable.
> Another reason is that technology is now part of education. Many schools use apps and online materials, so a child with no access is at a real disadvantage compared to classmates.
> That said, I completely understand the concerns. Too much screen time hurts sleep, attention and physical activity, and social media is clearly not appropriate for a ten-year-old.
> So my position is that the device isn't the problem — the rules around it are.
> That's why I'd say allow the phone, but control the apps and the hours."

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 7: Online classes vs. traditional classes

**Prompt:** *Do you think online classes are as effective as traditional classroom classes? Why or why not?*

> **Model (≈150 từ):**
> "In my opinion, online classes can be just as effective as traditional ones, but only for motivated adult learners.
> The main reason is flexibility. With online learning you can study at any time and repeat anything you didn't understand. For example, last year I took an online course about cloud architecture, and I studied for an hour every evening after my son went to sleep. There is simply no way I could have attended a physical class at that time.
> However, online learning has one serious weakness: nobody pushes you. In a classroom, the teacher notices when you're lost and other students keep you on schedule. At home, it's very easy to stop after two weeks, and most people do.
> So I'd say online classes are more effective for adults who already know why they're studying, while classrooms are better for younger students who still need structure.
> That's why I believe the answer depends on the learner, not the format."

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 8: Large company vs. small company

**Prompt:** *Some people prefer to work for a large company, while others prefer a small one. Which do you think is better, and why?*

> **Model (≈155 từ):**
> "In my opinion, a small company is better, at least early in your career.
> The main reason is how much you learn. In a small company you have to do many different things, so you grow much faster. For instance, in my first job there were only about fifteen people, and within a year I was writing code, talking directly to clients and even helping with hiring. A friend of mine joined a huge corporation at the same time and spent two years on one small part of one system.
> Another reason is impact. In a small team, your work is clearly visible and your ideas can actually change how the company operates, which is very motivating.
> Of course, large companies offer better salaries, more training budget and much more job security, and I understand why many people choose that.
> But personally, I'd still choose the small company, because I value learning and responsibility more than stability."

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 9: City life vs. countryside

**Prompt:** *Do you think it is better to live in a big city or in the countryside? Explain your opinion.*

> **Model (≈150 từ):**
> "In my opinion, living in a big city is better, mainly because of opportunity.
> The main reason is work. Almost all the good jobs in my industry are in Hanoi or Ho Chi Minh City, and if I moved to the countryside I'd have to change careers completely. Cities also give you far better schools, hospitals and universities, which matters enormously if you have children.
> Another reason is convenience and variety. In a city I can find anything I need within fifteen minutes, and there's always something to do — restaurants, cinemas, coffee shops, sports clubs.
> Of course, cities have real problems. The air is polluted, the traffic is exhausting and housing is expensive, so the countryside is definitely healthier and calmer.
> But at this stage of my life, career and education matter more to me than clean air.
> That's why I'd choose the city now, and perhaps the countryside after I retire."

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 10: Salary vs. job satisfaction

**Prompt:** *Some people say a high salary is the most important thing in a job. Do you agree or disagree?*

> **Model (≈150 từ):**
> "I disagree that salary is the most important thing, although I admit it matters a lot.
> The main reason is that money stops motivating you quite quickly. Once you earn enough to live comfortably and support your family, an extra ten percent doesn't really change your daily happiness. What does change it is whether you enjoy the work and respect the people around you.
> Another reason is the cost of a bad job. I've seen colleagues take much higher salaries at companies with terrible management, and most of them left within a year, exhausted and stressed. In my own experience, the happiest period of my career was not my highest-paid one — it was when I had a good manager and interesting problems to solve.
> Of course, nobody should work for a salary that doesn't cover their needs, so there's a minimum.
> But above that line, I believe satisfaction matters far more than the number."

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 11: Online meetings vs. face-to-face

**Prompt:** *Do you think online meetings are as effective as face-to-face meetings? Why?*

> **Model (≈150 từ):**
> "In my opinion, online meetings are effective for routine work, but not for everything.
> The main reason they work well is efficiency. Online meetings start on time, they're easy to schedule, and nobody wastes an hour travelling. For example, my team has a fifteen-minute standup online every morning, and it works perfectly — everyone shares their status and we finish quickly.
> However, online meetings are clearly weaker in two situations. The first is difficult conversations, like giving critical feedback, because you can't read people's body language properly. The second is creative discussion, where people need to interrupt each other and draw on a whiteboard; online, everyone waits politely and the energy disappears.
> For instance, when my team started a new project, we met in person for two days, and we made more progress than in three weeks of calls.
> So I'd say use online meetings for updates, and meet in person for anything difficult or creative."

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 12: The most important quality of a good leader

**Prompt:** *What do you think is the most important quality of a good manager? Give reasons and examples.*

> **Model (≈150 từ):**
> "In my opinion, the most important quality of a good manager is the ability to listen.
> The main reason is that a manager cannot possibly know everything about the work. The people doing the job every day see the problems first, and if the manager doesn't listen, those problems stay hidden until they become expensive. For example, in one of my projects, a junior developer warned us about a risk very early, and because we listened, we avoided a delay of several weeks.
> Another reason is trust. When people feel heard, they speak up honestly instead of hiding mistakes. In my own team, I try to ask questions before giving instructions, and I've noticed that people now report problems to me much earlier than they used to.
> Of course, a manager also needs technical knowledge and clear decision-making.
> But those are useless if nobody tells you the truth, so listening comes first."

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 13: Should employees have flexible working hours?

**Prompt:** *Some companies allow employees to choose their own working hours. Do you think this is a good idea?*

> **Model (≈150 từ):**
> "I strongly believe flexible working hours are a good idea for most office jobs.
> The main reason is productivity. People simply concentrate best at different times — some are sharpest at seven in the morning, others at ten at night. Forcing everyone into the same nine-to-five means many people spend their best hours commuting. In my own case, I do my most difficult technical work early in the morning, so a flexible start makes a real difference to how much I finish.
> Another reason is family life. Flexible hours let parents take their children to school or to the doctor without using a whole day of leave, and that reduces stress enormously.
> The obvious risk is coordination, because a team still needs to talk to each other. The usual solution is core hours — for instance, everyone must be available from ten to three.
> That's why I think flexibility works, as long as the team agrees on some shared time."

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 14: Is social media good or bad for society?

**Prompt:** *Some people think social media has a negative effect on society. What is your opinion?*

> **Model (≈155 từ):**
> "In my opinion, social media has serious negative effects, even though it also brings real benefits.
> The main reason for my concern is the effect on attention and mental health. These apps are designed to keep you scrolling, and most people, including me, spend far more time on them than they intended. Worse, people constantly compare their ordinary lives with other people's best moments, and that makes them feel dissatisfied.
> Another reason is the spread of false information. News travels in seconds without anyone checking whether it's true, and I've seen my own relatives believe things that were clearly fake.
> On the positive side, social media keeps families connected across long distances, and for small businesses it's a cheap and very effective way to find customers.
> So it isn't simple. But if I have to give one opinion, I'd say the harm currently outweighs the benefit, and platforms should be regulated more carefully."

## Q11 — Câu hỏi 15: Is it better to buy or rent a home?

**Prompt:** *Do you think it is better to buy a house or to rent one? Give reasons for your answer.*

> **Model (≈150 từ):**
> "In my opinion, buying a house is better in the long run, especially for people with families.
> The main reason is financial security. When you rent, your money is gone every month and you own nothing at the end. When you buy, each payment builds something you keep, and in my country property prices have risen steadily for decades. My parents bought their house thirty years ago, and it's now worth many times what they paid.
> Another reason is stability. As an owner, nobody can ask you to move out, which matters a lot when children are settled in a nearby school.
> That said, renting is clearly better for young people who might change city or change job, because it keeps them flexible and doesn't require a huge deposit.
> So my view is that you should rent while your life is still changing, and buy once you know where you want to stay."

## 🔧 Bộ khung tái dùng cho Q11

| Khối | Câu mẫu |
|---|---|
| **Nêu lập trường** | *"In my opinion, …"* · *"I strongly believe that …"* · *"I completely agree that …"* · *"I partly agree, but overall …"* |
| **Lý do 1** | *"The main reason is …"* · *"The most important reason is …"* |
| **Ví dụ** | *"For example, …"* · *"For instance, …"* · *"In my own experience, …"* · *"In my case, …"* |
| **Lý do 2** | *"Another reason is …"* · *"On top of that, …"* · *"Secondly, …"* |
| **Nhượng bộ** | *"That said, …"* · *"Of course, …"* · *"I do understand the other side …"* |
| **Kết** | *"That's why I believe …"* · *"So overall, I'd say …"* · *"For these reasons, …"* |

### Bộ lý do vạn năng — dùng cho hầu hết mọi đề

`time` · `money / cost` · `health` · `stress` · `flexibility` · `quality` · `career opportunity` · `relationships` · `safety` · `learning`

> Đọc đề → quét danh sách này → chọn 2 cái hợp nhất → xong 20 giây chuẩn bị.

### Bẫy giết điểm Q11
1. **Không chốt phe / đổi phe giữa chừng** → chốt ngay câu đầu, giữ đến hết.
2. **Có lý do nhưng không có ví dụ** → mỗi lý do phải kèm một ví dụ cụ thể.
3. **Hết giờ trước khi kết** → nói lý do gọn, chừa 10 giây cuối cho câu kết.
4. **Nói quá ít (dưới 100 từ)** → bị trừ vì "thiếu triển khai", dù nói đúng hết.
5. **Lặp lại cùng một ý bằng từ khác** → bị trừ y như nói thiếu.
